# Anonymized SUS/TAM Results

**Source:** `Advisor Log Responses.xlsx`, `Form Responses 1`  
**Participants:** U01–U10  
**Collection dates:** 2–4 November 2026

The accompanying [`sus-tam-results.csv`](sus-tam-results.csv) contains the anonymized item-level Likert responses and calculated construct scores. It does not contain names, email addresses, consent fields, timestamps, or open-ended comments.

## Anonymization and scope

The source participant-code field was blank. U01–U10 are assigned sequentially to the ten questionnaire rows in source order: U01–U08 are Student respondents and U09–U10 are Advisor respondents. The one QA UAT participant did not submit a SUS/TAM questionnaire and is therefore not represented in this file.

The file contains one questionnaire set collected after participants evaluated V1 and V2. It is **not** a separate V1 score and V2 score. SUS/TAM values must therefore be reported as results from the combined V1/V2 evaluation, not as a numerical V1-to-V2 improvement.

## Scoring

- SUS odd items use `response - 1`; even items use `5 - response`; the total is multiplied by 2.5.
- PU is the mean of PU1–PU3.
- PEOU is the mean of PEOU1–PEOU3.
- BI is the mean of BI1–BI3.

The anonymized CSV reproduces the reported aggregates:

| Measure | Overall result |
|---|---:|
| SUS | 64.00 / 100 |
| PU | 4.33 / 5 |
| PEOU | 4.17 / 5 |
| BI | 4.13 / 5 |

These results support the reported questionnaire summary but do not establish population-wide usability, a separate V1/V2 SUS/TAM change, or a statistically significant improvement.
