## Reflection

### 1. Lessons Learned

Building AdvisingLog with AI improved my speed in drafting test cases, organizing UAT evidence, and explaining SUS/TAM calculations. However, I learned that AI output must always be checked against code, test results, datasets, and Git history. Faster development does not replace requirements analysis, privacy review, regression testing, or teamwork. AI helped me work more efficiently, but human judgment was still necessary to detect unsupported claims.
